How it works

A UR (Uniform Resource) is how air-gapped wallets hand data — seeds, PSBTs, key descriptors — to a connected device. Large payloads do not fit in a single QR code, so they are split into fragments, bundled into a fountain code, and animated as a stream of QR frames.

  1. Each frame is a self-describing ur:<type>/<seq>/<bytewords> string.
  2. Frames may arrive in any order, repeat, or be missed — the fountain code recovers the whole message from roughly n of any n+few frames.
  3. Decoding continues until the bytewords and message checksums agree, then the payload is shown.

Decoding uses @ngraveio/bc-ur against the BCR-2020-005 specification. Camera frames never leave the browser tab.

Hardware-wallet payloads often contain secret material. Treat anything decoded here accordingly, and only use devices you trust.
